TWO-PLAYER CARD DUEL
A simple ruleset for two players.

SETUP
1. Use a standard 52-card deck.
2. Shuffle the deck and deal 5 cards to each player.
3. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
4. Flip the top card of the draw pile to start the discard pile.
5. The player with the lowest first card goes first. Ace is high.

TURN STRUCTURE
1. On your turn, draw 1 card from the draw pile.
2. You may play 1 card from your hand onto the discard pile if it matches the suit or rank of the top discard.
3. If you cannot play a card, you must draw 1 additional card and your turn ends.
4. After playing a card, you may attack once by discarding any card of the same color as the card you just played.
5. Players may only attack once per turn.
6. If you attack, you must attack again immediately.

SCORING AND COMBAT
1. Red cards deal 1 damage.
2. Black cards deal 2 damage.
3. Face cards (J, Q, K) deal 3 damage.
4. Aces deal 1 damage.
5. Each player starts with 10 life.
6. When a player takes damage equal to or greater than their remaining life, they are eliminated.

SPECIAL CARDS
1. Playing a 2 lets you skip the other player's next turn.
2. Playing a 2 also forces the other player to draw 2 cards immediately.
3. Queens reverse turn order.
4. Since this is a two-player game, Queens instead let you take an extra turn.
5. Jokers are not used.

WINNING
1. Reduce the other player to 0 life to win.
2. If the draw pile runs out, reshuffle the discard pile to form a new draw pile.
3. If both players reach 0 life on the same attack, the player who attacked wins.
4. If both players reach 0 life on the same attack, the game is a draw.

NOTES
- Players must always have at least 1 card in hand. If a player would discard their last card while attacking, they cannot attack.
- Players may hold a maximum of 7 cards. If you ever have more than 7 cards at the end of your turn, discard down to 5.
